A Poem For My Wifeyy 📝 — Every line belongs to you

🌸 🌹 ✨ 💗
Since 27/09/2007 · Nineteen Years of Magic 💌

To My Dearest Wifeyy,

First time nee photo chusina aa day,

My heart just stopped in a beautiful way.

Oka photo anukunna, just a face on the screen,

Kaani naa heart lo appude, "She's the one" ani feeling.

"Thaney kavali..." ani heart lo anukunna,

Why I felt that way, naake ardham kaaledanna.

It was just a picture, just one little view,

But somehow my heart already belonged to you.

Tarvatha school ki vachav, ninnu real ga chusa,

Aa moment lo naa heart tho nene maatladukusa.

Photo lo kanna real ga inka beautiful ga unnav,

Aa second lone naa world motham nuvvayipoyav.

I thought you might say no, I was scared you would go,

Kaani nuvvu yes annav, and my heart started to glow.

I never imagined someone could love me this way,

But you chose to stay with me, every single day.

Nannu chala ante chala baaga chusukunnav,

Naa happiness kosam nee happiness kuda marchipoyav.

Naku inko mommy laaga naa pakkana nilichav,

Em problem rakunda nannu prathi roju kapadav.

Naku em kavalo cheppakundane telusukunnav,

Naa little little wishes anni heart tho neraverchav.

Naa kosam chesina things I can never repay,

Because you loved me more in your own special way.

Nenu thelika enno thappulu chesina sare,

Naa meeda kopam vachina, still nuvvu vellaledu vere.

Prathi sari nannu kshaminchi, naa pakkane nilichav,

Nenu maarali ani korukuntu, naa cheyyi pattukunnav.

Nijaniki nuvvu oka devatha naa life lo vachav,

Naa broken pieces anni prema tho kalipav.

Nee valle nenu ila unnanu, nee valle maaranu,

Nuvvu chupinchina love valle, nenu nannu kanugonnanu.

Nannu ni la evaru love cheyaru, I know it's true,

Because no one in this world could ever be you.

Naa life lo dorikina greatest gift nuvve,

Naa heart lo forever undalsina person nuvve.

I love you, cutiee more than words can say,

Nuvvu lekunda naa life lo meaning ledu ani I pray.

Naku nuvvu just today kaadu, life long kavali,

Every tomorrow, every lifetime, naa pakkana nuvve undali.

If life gives me a thousand chances to choose,

Every single time, I would never refuse.

Again I would find you, again I would choose you,

Because in every life, my heart will still choose you. ❤️

Forever & Always Yours,

your's vinnyy ❤️
